# 1. Executive Summary

Dhanalaxmi Smart Customer Portal is a mobile-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) that transforms a simple QR code into a complete customer engagement platform.

Instead of directing customers only to a Google Review page, the portal provides multiple customer services from one interface, including:

- Google Review guidance
- Private customer feedback
- Loyalty program
- Today's offers
- Store information
- Contact details
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Future digital services

The application is designed to improve customer experience while helping businesses increase engagement and gather valuable insights.

The architecture is modular, scalable, and configurable, allowing the same platform to support multiple businesses in the future.

# 4. Problem Statement

Most businesses place a Google Review QR code near the billing counter.

Customers scan the QR code but often abandon the process because they:

- Don't know what Google Reviews are.
- Don't know how to sign in.
- Don't know where to click.
- Don't know what to write.
- Prefer local languages.
- Fear making mistakes.

As a result:

- Low review conversion
- Missed customer feedback
- Poor customer engagement
- Lost marketing opportunities

# 11. Guiding Principles

The platform will be designed around the following principles:

### Simplicity

Every screen should have a clear purpose and avoid unnecessary complexity.

### Accessibility

Support users of different ages, languages, and technical abilities.

### Performance

Fast loading and responsive interactions, even on lower-end smartphones.

### Modularity

Each feature (Reviews, Offers, Loyalty, Feedback, etc.) should be independently maintainable and expandable.

### Scalability

The platform must support additional businesses through configuration rather than code changes.

### Security

Protect business data and follow secure development practices.

### Privacy

Collect only the information necessary for the application's functionality and analytics.

### Compliance

Follow applicable platform policies, including Google Business Profile review guidelines.
